# Clarification

When facts are missing, ask before inventing. Do not block a draft behind a long questionnaire.

## When to draft immediately

If **brand + creator + collaboration intent** are present, draft now. Extract name and platform from profile or video URLs. Do not re-ask known facts.

Unknown tier, fee, or content hook is not a blocker: draft, keep `{{placeholders}}` where needed, and list them under Assumptions.

## When to ask

Ask only for **blocking** gaps — things that would make the email wrong if guessed.

Cap: **1–3 questions** in one turn, then draft.

Typical blocking questions (pick at most three):

1. Paid, product seeding, affiliate, or paid + affiliate?
2. Reply, book a call, or open a brief link?
3. One piece of content you actually watched (title + a concrete detail)?
4. Writing the creator, or their manager / MCN?
5. Fee range, if this is a paid or hybrid pitch and the user expects a number in the body?

Do not ask for follower count, calendar links, or exclusivity on a first cold draft unless the user is already in the terms stage.

## Field map

**Fill or placeholder:**

- Brand / product and 1–2 sentence value proposition
- Creator name, platform, content style
- Follower count or estimated tier (Nano / Micro / Mid / Macro)
- Collab type: paid / seeding / affiliate / paid + affiliate / ongoing
- Recipient: creator vs manager / MCN
- CTA: reply, call, or link

**Optional (omit or placeholder):**

- Fee range
- Affiliate commission and cookie window (do not invent the cookie)
- A real content hook
- Sender name, title, company, email, calendar link
- Deliverables, publish window, usage rights (terms stage only)

Never invent fees, video titles, follower counts, deadlines, or legal terms.

## After they answer

Incorporate the answers, drop the matching `{{placeholders}}`, and send Subject + body. If they skip a question, keep the placeholder and note it under Assumptions — do not stall.
