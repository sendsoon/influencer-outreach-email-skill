# Subject Lines

Subject lines decide open rate. Creator inboxes are full of PR blasts. Write a short, specific note from a real person.

## Rules

- Prefer **40–60 characters**; >70 truncates on mobile.
- Include at least two of: creator, brand, content theme, collab type.
- Noun + purpose. Few adjectives.
- First email: no `Re:`, `Fwd:`, or `Follow-up:` (reserve Follow-up for later touches).
- One subject per draft. List an alternate on its own line — not as an A/B essay.

## Do Not Use

Keep these out of the subject and preview text:

`FREE` · `$$$` · `ACT NOW` · `URGENT` · `LIMITED TIME` · `100%` · `WINNER` · `CONGRATULATIONS` · `GUARANTEED` · `NO OBLIGATION` · `CLICK HERE` · `MAKE MONEY` · `GIVEAWAY` (unless the user confirmed that offer)

Also avoid: ALL CAPS, stacked exclamation marks, emoji piles, `Opportunity!!!`, `Exciting collab`, `Let's work together!`.

## Templates by Stage

### Cold outreach

1. `{{creator_name}} — idea for a {{brand_name}} collab`
2. `Quick note on {{content_hook}} + {{brand_name}}`
3. `{{brand_name}} × {{creator_name}}: {{collab_type}}`
4. `Collaboration around {{content_theme}}`
5. `Sending over a {{collab_type}} idea`
6. `{{content_hook}} — a fit for {{brand_name}}?`
7. `Paid integration idea for {{handle}}`

### Follow-up

8. `Following up: {{brand_name}} × {{creator_name}}`
9. `Checking in on the {{brand_name}} idea`
10. `Still relevant: {{collab_type}} with {{brand_name}}`
11. `Closing the loop — {{brand_name}}`

### Terms

12. `{{brand_name}} collab details for review`
13. `Confirming deliverables and timeline — {{brand_name}}`
14. `Draft terms: {{creator_name}} × {{brand_name}}`

### Seeding / shipping

15. `{{brand_name}} sample shipped — tracking inside`
16. `Package on the way + suggested unboxing notes`

## Filled Examples

| Scene | Subject |

| YouTube paid integration | `Maya — idea for a Northstar collab` |

| Follow-up #2 | `Following up: Northstar × Maya` |
| Terms | `Confirming deliverables and timeline — Northstar` |

## Quick Test

1. Strip brand and creator names. If nothing specific remains, rewrite.
2. If it could pass as a newsletter, add a content hook.
3. Delete discount, “free,” or fake urgency.
